## Important boundaries

- Files, formats, and download channels can change and are not guaranteed to remain available permanently.
- The current workflow does not automatically determine copyright ownership.
- The detailed status and policy documents are maintained in Chinese because ZhihaiLib primarily serves Chinese readers.
- Confidential rights or security reports must not be posted as public GitHub issues.
